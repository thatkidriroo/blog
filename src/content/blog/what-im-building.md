One of my favorite things is hearing what people are working on. There's something special about that raw, in-progress energy — before everything is polished and presentable.

So here's what's on my plate right now.

### This blog

Meta, I know. But building this blog has been genuinely fun. It's built with [Astro](https://astro.build), which I've been really enjoying. Lightweight, fast, and writing content in Markdown just feels right.

I wanted a space that was mine — no algorithms, no distractions, just words on a page.

### A CLI tool I'm hacking on

I've been building a little CLI tool in Python. Nothing revolutionary — it helps me scaffold out project templates faster. But building it has taught me a ton about argparse, file system operations, and why good dev experience matters.

I'll probably open-source it once it's less ugly.

### Reading more

Not a project in the traditional sense, but I've been trying to read more consistently. Currently making my way through *The Psychology of Money* by Morgan Housel. It's one of those books where every chapter makes you stop and think for a minute.
