I love reading other people's "favorites" posts — they feel like a little time capsule. So here's mine for this season.

### 🎵 Music

I've been looping *Scenery* by Ryo Fukui on repeat. It's jazz piano from the 70s and it hits different. Perfect for coding, reading, or just staring out the window.

### 📖 Book

*The Psychology of Money* by Morgan Housel. I know, I mentioned it in my last post too — it's just that good. It's not about math or spreadsheets. It's about how we think about money, and honestly, it applies to life in general.

### 🎮 Game

I've been playing *Stardew Valley* again. There's something comforting about tending to a farm, fishing, and befriending pixel villagers. It's my cozy escape after a long day.

### ☕ Drink

I recently got into pour-over coffee. Started with a simple V60 setup. There's a ritual to it that I love — boiling water, grinding beans, the slow pour. It forces me to slow down for 3 minutes. Worth it.

### 🎧 Podcast

*The Ezra Klein Show*. Every episode makes me think differently about something. His interview style is genuinely curious, not combative. Refreshing.
