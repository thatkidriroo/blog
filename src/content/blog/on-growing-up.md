I don't think anyone tells you how weird your twenties are.

One day you feel like you have it together. You're paying bills on time, eating vegetables, showing up for your friends. The next day you're lying on the floor at 11pm wondering what you're doing with your life.

And both of those days are normal.

### The pressure to have it figured out

There's this unspoken timeline we all feel. By X age you should have Y job, Z relationship, and at least a half-baked 5-year plan. But life doesn't work like that. It's messy, nonlinear, and full of plot twists.

I'm learning to be okay with not having it all figured out. To enjoy the not-knowing. To treat life less like a checklist and more like an exploration.

### What's actually helped

A few things that have genuinely made a difference:

- **Therapy** — Should've started earlier. Full stop.
- **Journaling** — Just 5 minutes a day. Gets the noise out of my head.
- **Saying no more often** — To plans I don't want, to obligations that drain me, to people who don't pour into me.
- **Calling my mom** — She likes it more than texting, and honestly, so do I.

### The takeaway

I don't have a grand conclusion. I'm still figuring it out. But I think that's the point — we're all just figuring it out together.

If you're in the same boat, hi. Let's figure it out.
