I've been meaning to start a blog for a while now. Every time I sat down to write, I'd overthink it — what if nobody reads it? What if it's not good enough? What if I run out of things to say?

Then I realized: this isn't for "an audience." It's for my friends, and for future me.

I wanted a place where I could share what I'm learning, thinking about, and building — without the pressure of making it perfect. A digital campfire, if you will. Cozy, honest, and real.

So here it is. This little corner of the internet where I get to be myself.

### What to expect

Honestly? A bit of everything. Some posts will be about things I'm coding or building. Others will be life updates, lessons I've learned the hard way, or just random thoughts I had while walking home.

I'm not gonna box myself into one topic. That's not how life works, and it's not how this blog will work either.

### Thanks for stopping by

If you're reading this — friend, stranger, or my future self — thanks. I hope you find something here that resonates, makes you laugh, or at least makes you think.

See you around.
