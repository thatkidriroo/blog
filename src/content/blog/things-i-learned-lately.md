I keep a notes app dump of things I learn — not just technical stuff, but life things too. Here are a few that have been stuck in my head lately.

### Doing > perfecting

I spent way too long waiting until I was "ready" before putting things out there. Ready for what? Ready for who? The truth is, you never feel ready. You just start, and you figure it out along the way.

This blog is proof of that. I could've spent another month polishing the design, tweaking the CSS, rewriting the about page. Instead, I just published it. Feels good.

### Consistency beats intensity

I used to think productivity was about having these massive, intense work sessions. 8 hours. No breaks. Grind mode. But I've learned that showing up a little bit every day — even just 30 minutes — adds up way more than one heroic all-nighter.

Small steps, regular rhythm. That's the move.

### Rest isn't a reward

This one hit me hard. I used to treat rest like something I had to earn. "I'll rest after I finish this project." "I'll take a break when I hit this milestone." But rest isn't a reward for productivity — it's a requirement for it.

Taking a real break, guilt-free, makes everything else better.

### People remember how you made them feel

I've been trying to be more intentional about this. Sending that check-in text. Remembering the little things. Showing up. In a world where everyone's busy, just being present is a superpower.
